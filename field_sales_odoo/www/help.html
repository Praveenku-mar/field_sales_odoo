Field Sales Help

{% endblock %} {% block page_content %}

Field Sales is for brands that sell to retail shops through a field team. Reps plan visits, check in at the shop with GPS and take orders against your Shopify stock; the orders and retailers are created in your Shopify store. Managers use Field Sales inside the Shopify admin; reps use the Field Sales app on their phones.

Requirements

  • A Shopify store with at least one location that holds stock, and products with prices.
  • Reps need a smartphone with a current browser (Chrome or Safari) and location turned on.
  • An outgoing email account for welcome, password and order confirmation emails (see Locations, order mode and email).
  • Optional: a Razorpay account for payment links (India only).

Getting started

  1. Install Field Sales from the Shopify App Store and approve the requested access. Field Sales opens inside your Shopify admin, connects your store, picks your first location as the default and starts syncing products and stock.
  2. Open Settings to check the locations and the order mode, and set up outgoing email (next section).
  3. Add your sales reps (Sales reps). They sign in to the rep app on their phones.
  4. Plan visits, or let reps take orders on their visits.

Locations, order mode and email

Open Settings in Field Sales inside your Shopify admin. It shows the connected shop, the access you granted and your plan, and lets you change:

  • Locations: Fetch Locations loads your Shopify locations; tick Sell from here for each one reps may sell from, and choose the default. Changing the default starts a product sync.
  • Order mode: Draft creates a draft order in Shopify that your team reviews and completes; Confirmed creates a real order straight away.
  • Product sync: every 30 minutes, hourly or daily.
  • Email Account: the outgoing email account used for welcome emails, password links and order confirmations (for Gmail, use an app password).
  • Invoice: company details printed on the invoice (and GSTIN in India), and whether an order confirmation email is sent.
  • Commission: turn commission on or off for your company.
  • Currency and time zone follow your Shopify store.

Sales reps

  1. Go to Sales Reps and click New Sales Rep.
  2. Enter the rep's email and name, choose who they report to, and keep Send welcome email on.
  3. The rep gets an email to set a password. If it's lost, open the rep and click Send Reset Link.

Reps see only their own customers, visits and orders; a manager sees everyone who reports to them. You can set a monthly target and a maximum discount per rep.

The rep app

  • Reps open /fieldsales on your Field Sales site in their phone's browser and sign in with the email and password from the welcome email.
  • Add it to the home screen (Chrome: menu → Install app; Safari: Share → Add to Home Screen) to open it like an app.
  • GPS check-in and barcode scanning need the phone's location and camera, and work only in the rep app, not inside the Shopify admin.

Products and stock

  • Products, variants, prices, barcodes and stock are copied from Shopify on the chosen schedule; stock and price changes in Shopify also arrive within moments.
  • Stock shown to reps is for the order's location. When an order is submitted, stock is checked live in Shopify again; an item over the available stock stops the order.
  • Products are changed only in Shopify; Field Sales does not edit them.

Customers

  • Retail shops are Customers in Field Sales. Each is created as a customer in Shopify, with its phone, email and address.
  • The country sets how the phone number is checked; new customers start in your store's country.
  • A customer's location (map pin) is used for the check-in distance. It is set on the first check-in or picked on the map; after that only a manager can move it.

Visits and check-in

  • Managers plan visits for reps one by one or by importing an Excel file (Visits → Import).
  • Only the assigned rep can check in, and only on the visit's date. Check-in records the GPS position and the distance to the shop (within 50 m by default; managers can change a shop's radius).
  • At check-out the rep records whether an order was taken, or why not.

Orders

  • Reps take orders on a visit or from a customer, with quantities, discount (up to their limit) and tax.
  • On submit, the order is sent to Shopify as a draft or confirmed order (your order mode), with the Field Sales order number and the rep on it.
  • The retailer gets an order confirmation email with an invoice PDF (if switched on).
  • To change a submitted order, cancel it and create a new one.

Payments (Razorpay, India)

  • Enter your Razorpay keys under Settings → Razorpay.
  • A payment link is added to the confirmation email, and can be copied or shared on WhatsApp from the order.
  • When the retailer pays, the order shows Paid, and confirmed orders are marked paid in Shopify.

Sync failures and retrying

A sync failure means Shopify refused, or could not be reached for, a customer or an order. Sync Failures lists each one with Shopify's message.

  • Common causes: an item out of stock, a location that no longer exists, an email address Shopify rejects, or too many orders sent at once.
  • Fix the cause (edit the customer or order, or the product in Shopify), then click Retry or Retry All.
  • Failed orders are also retried automatically every 15 minutes, up to 5 times.
  • Sync Log shows every call to Shopify, for troubleshooting.

Commission basics

  • Create a commission plan (flat %, per product, tiered slabs or per order) and assign it to reps. Managers can also earn on their team's sales.
  • Commission is earned when the Shopify order is paid or fulfilled (chosen per plan), and reversed if it is cancelled or refunded.
  • Monthly statements are drafted on the 1st; a manager approves them and marks them paid.
  • Companies that don't pay commission can turn it off (Settings → Commission).

Plans

  • You choose your Field Sales plan on Shopify's plan page; charges appear on your Shopify bill. Settings shows your current plan and usage.
  • If a plan limits the number of reps or orders per month, Field Sales tells you when the limit is reached, with a link to change your plan.

Troubleshooting

Products or stock are missing.
Check that a default location is set in Settings; setting it starts a sync. Only active products with a price are shown.
"Location is not available inside Shopify admin".
Browsers don't give location to apps inside the Shopify admin. Use Open in Field Sales on the visit, or the rep app on a phone.
A rep didn't get the welcome email.
Check the spam folder and that outgoing email is set up, then use Send Reset Link on the rep.
Check-in is not offered.
Check-in is only for the assigned rep, on the visit's date, in the rep app.
An order is stuck as Pending or Failed.
Open Sync Failures for Shopify's message; see Sync failures.

Uninstalling and data deletion

  • Uninstalling removes Field Sales' access to your store at once, and syncing stops.
  • 48 hours later Shopify asks us to delete your shop's data: customers' personal data is anonymised, synced products and log contents are deleted. Order amounts and dates are kept anonymised for your records.
  • Requests from your shoppers to see or delete their data are handled automatically through Shopify. See the privacy policy for details.
