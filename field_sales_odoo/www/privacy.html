Last updated: 23 October 2026

Field Sales ("the app") is provided by [TO FILL: legal name of the company] ("we", "us"). It helps a brand's field sales team visit retail shops, take orders and sync those orders to the brand's online store. This policy explains what personal data the app processes for a merchant (the brand that installs the app), why, where it is stored and for how long. The merchant decides which data is entered; we process it on the merchant's behalf.

1. Data we process

  • Retailers (the merchant's business customers): shop name, owner or contact person name, email address, phone number, postal address, country, tax number (GSTIN, India only) and the shop's map location (latitude and longitude).
  • Visits: planned and completed visits by sales reps, including the GPS position and accuracy captured at check-in and check-out, the distance to the shop, notes and an optional photo.
  • Orders: items, quantities, prices, discounts, taxes, delivery dates, remarks, the store order and draft numbers, and the sync status with the store.
  • Payments: when the merchant uses Razorpay payment links, the link id and URL, payment status, amount paid and payment id. We do not receive or store card or bank details.
  • Sales rep users: name, email address, mobile number, territory, reporting line, targets, commission plan and commission amounts, and sign-in records.
  • Shopify staff: when the app is opened inside the Shopify admin, the Shopify staff member's numeric user id from the session token, used for audit logs.
  • Technical logs: records of calls to the store and to Razorpay. Secrets and customer contact details (names, emails, phone numbers, addresses) are masked before a log is saved.

2. Why we process it

  • To let reps plan and record visits, and check that a visit happened at the shop (geofence check).
  • To create and update the retailer as a customer in the merchant's online store, and to place orders there.
  • To send order confirmations, invoices and payment links to retailers.
  • To calculate sales rep commission and show managers reports on visits and orders.
  • To find and fix sync errors (technical logs).

We do not sell personal data, use it for advertising, or make automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects on retailers or reps.

3. Where data is stored

Each merchant's data is stored in its own database on [TO FILL: hosting provider] in [TO FILL: region/country]. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest [TO FILL: confirm disk and backup encryption with the hosting provider]. Store access tokens and API keys are stored encrypted.

4. How long we keep it

  • Retailers, visits, orders and commission records: while the merchant uses the app, unless the merchant deletes them. The merchant can switch on automatic anonymisation of inactive retailers after a chosen number of days.
  • Successful technical logs: 30 days. Failed technical logs: 180 days by default (the merchant can change this).
  • After the merchant uninstalls the app from Shopify, the store access tokens are deleted immediately. When Shopify sends its shop deletion request (48 hours after uninstall), retailer personal data is anonymised, product data and log contents are deleted, and data exports are removed. 6. Rights of retailers and other individuals

Retailers and reps can ask the merchant to see, correct or delete their personal data. For shoppers and customers of a Shopify store, Shopify forwards data requests to us:

  • Customer data request: we export what we hold about that customer (retailer details, visits and orders) to a file, and notify the merchant's administrators to send it to the store owner within 30 days.
  • Customer deletion request: we anonymise the customer's name, contact details, address and location on the retailer and its orders and visits, and delete the edit history and emails that held them. Amounts and dates are kept.
  • Shop deletion request: see section 4.

We respond within 30 days, unless the law requires us to keep the data.
